What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting digital devices, networks, and data from unauthorized access, attacks, or damage. it uses tools like firewalls, strong passwords, and encryption to keep hackers from stealing sensitive personal information, compromising financial systems, or disrupting essential services online.
