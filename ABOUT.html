Why Cybersecurity is Important

Cybersecurity is important because so much of our daily lives happens online. Without protection, hackers can steal your money, lock you out of your accounts, or access private photos and messages. Staying secure keeps your personal data safe, and ensures the apps and websites you rely on work safely.
