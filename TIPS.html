Top 5 Cybersecurity Tips

1. Use a Strong Password

Always create passwords that are at least 12 characters long. Use a mix of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special symbols. Avoid using easily guessable information like birthdays or pet names.

2. Do Not Share Your Password

Your password is the key to your digital life. Never write it down on sticky notes attached to your monitor, and do not share it with friends, family, or co-workers.

3. Do Not Click Suspicious Links

Phishing is a common way hackers steal information. If you receive an unexpected email or text message urging you to click a link to verify an account or claim a prize, ignore it. Always verify the source first.

4. Keep Your Software Updated

Software updates often include patches for newly discovered security vulnerabilities. Turn on automatic updates for your operating system, web browser, and antivirus software.

5. Use Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Whenever possible, enable 2FA on your accounts. This requires a secondary form of identification, like a code sent to your phone, making it much harder for hackers to log in even if they know your password.
